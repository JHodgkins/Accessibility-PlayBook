# Focus states

**Audience:** Designers, Product, QA  
**Purpose:** Make keyboard location easy to perceive and understand.

## Core guidance

- Interactive controls must have a visible focus indicator.
- Focus styling should remain visible against adjacent backgrounds.
- Do not remove browser focus indication unless an accessible replacement is provided.

## Handoff expectation

Designs should communicate relevant states and expected behaviour clearly enough that
development and QA do not need to infer the accessibility requirement after build.

## Specify focus across states

Show the keyboard focus treatment for default, selected, error and dark-background contexts. A selected tab still needs a separate focus indicator. Check the outline is not clipped by overflow or covered by sticky headers. Ask engineering to demonstrate backwards as well as forwards keyboard travel.

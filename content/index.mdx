## How the pieces fit together

The playbook connects organisational intent with practical delivery:

1. **Standards** establish the accessibility baseline.
2. **The operating model** defines who is responsible and when accessibility work happens.
3. **Design and development guidance** help teams prevent defects.
4. **Component specifications** define expected accessible behaviour.
5. **Testing guidance** explains how to verify that behaviour.
6. **Remediation guidance** helps teams prioritise and fix failures.
7. **Delivery controls** connect accessibility to acceptance, release, risk and reporting.
8. **Resources** make the approach repeatable through templates, tools and checklists.

## Playbook principles

- Accessibility is a **shared delivery responsibility**, supported by specialists.
- Preventing defects is preferable to finding them late.
- Automated testing is useful, but **does not replace manual evaluation**.
- Native semantics are preferred before ARIA.
- Severity is driven by **user impact and task impact**, not only by the WCAG level attached to a defect.
- Guidance should be reusable, role-aware and easy to maintain.

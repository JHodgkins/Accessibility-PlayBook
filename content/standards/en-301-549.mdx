# EN 301 549

EN 301 549 defines accessibility requirements for information and communication
technology and is particularly relevant in European procurement and regulatory
contexts.

The playbook should identify applicable EN 301 549 requirements where they affect the
product or service, while keeping detailed normative interpretation in specialist
standards guidance.

## Version used in this playbook

The component mappings explicitly use
[EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03), official ETSI PDF](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/03.02.01_60/en_301549v030201p.pdf).
Its chapter 9 web requirements reference WCAG 2.1. WCAG 2.2 additions are therefore
identified separately; do not infer EN clauses by prefixing every WCAG 2.2 number.

This versioned mapping does not assert which edition governs a particular contract
or jurisdiction. Procurement and legal/risk must establish that applicability.
Other EN requirements can apply to documents, software, hardware and support services;
passing the selected web checks is not a whole-product EN conformance claim.

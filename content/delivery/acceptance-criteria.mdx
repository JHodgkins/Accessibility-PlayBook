# Accessibility acceptance criteria

**Audience:** Product owners, business analysts, designers, developers and QA  
**Purpose:** Agree observable outcomes before implementation.  
**When to use:** Refinement, handoff, evaluation and acceptance.  
**Owner:** Product / QA

Good acceptance criteria describe user-observable or testable outcomes rather than
simply saying “meets WCAG”.

## Form field example

- Given I navigate by keyboard, when focus reaches the field, then the field has a visible focus indicator.
- Given I use a screen reader, when focus reaches the field, then I can determine its label and required state.
- When validation fails, the visible error is programmatically associated with the affected field.
- At 400% zoom, the field, label, instructions and error remain usable without loss of essential information.

## Modal example

- When the modal opens, focus moves to a documented logical target inside it.
- The modal exposes an accessible name.
- Keyboard navigation cannot move into obscured background controls while the modal is active.
- When the modal closes, focus returns to a logical location.

## Template

Use:

> **Given** [user/context], **when** [interaction/state], **then** [observable accessible outcome].

Criteria should be specific enough that product, engineering and QA can agree on the
same expected result.

## Agree and verify criteria

Each criterion must identify the interaction state and how it will be evaluated.
Avoid treating one component criterion as coverage for the full journey. Include
loading, invalid input, cancellation and recovery as well as successful completion.

| Criterion | Verification | Evidence owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invalid email can be found and corrected without a pointer | Submit, navigate summary, edit, resubmit | QA |
| Dialog cancellation preserves the current task | Open, cancel, check data and focus | Developer and QA |
| All actions remain reachable when enlarged | 200% and 400% zoom with keyboard traversal | QA |

During refinement, QA should demonstrate that each criterion has a repeatable test.
At acceptance, Product reviews linked results and unresolved defects. A blocked test
must remain visible in the [release report](/delivery/reporting).
See [form fields](/components/form-fields), [modal dialogs](/components/modal-dialogs)
and the [test record](/resources/templates#method-test-record).

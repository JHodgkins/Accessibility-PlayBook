# Accessibility risk acceptance

**Audience:** Product, delivery, accessibility, legal/risk and authorised approvers  
**Purpose:** Record a temporary business decision without concealing the barrier.  
**When to use:** A known defect cannot be fixed before a proposed release.  
**Owner:** Authorised risk owner

A risk record should capture:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Barrier | What fails |
| Affected users | Who experiences the problem |
| Journey | Which task is affected |
| Impact | What the user cannot do or must do differently |
| Workaround | Whether a realistic workaround exists |
| Reason for exception | Why it cannot be fixed before release |
| Owner | Who is accountable for remediation |
| Target date | When remediation is expected |
| Approver | Who accepts the release risk |
| Review date | When the decision is reconsidered |

Risk acceptance does not change whether a barrier exists. It records an accountable
business decision about unresolved risk.

## Decision rules

The proposer must include alternatives considered and evidence for any workaround.
Accessibility and QA advise on user impact; the named risk owner accepts only within
their delegated authority. Legal obligations require appropriate legal review and
cannot be waived by changing a ticket status.

Keep the defect open, assign funded remediation work and record both review and expiry
dates. Reassess immediately when scope, severity or workaround reliability changes.
Do not renew an exception automatically. A repeated exception must trigger a systemic
review of the component, supplier or delivery process.

Use the [worked expiry example](/operating-model/escalation-and-exceptions).
At closure, link retest evidence and the closing decision. At expiry without a fix,
apply the [release gate](/delivery/release-gates) and escalate to the accountable owner.

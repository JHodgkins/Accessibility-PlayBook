# Purpose and scope

**Audience:** Everyone  
**Purpose:** Explain the organisational problem this playbook solves.  
**When to use:** When introducing the playbook, onboarding a team or deciding whether guidance belongs here.  
**Owner:** Accessibility capability / product governance

## Why this exists

Accessibility knowledge often lives in separate audit reports, test scripts, design
notes and individual specialists. That makes good practice difficult to scale.

This playbook creates a shared operational reference that connects accessibility to
the way products are already designed, built, tested and released.

## In scope

The POC covers:

- web-focused design and engineering guidance;
- component behaviour;
- keyboard, screen-reader, magnification and mobile considerations;
- testing and remediation;
- delivery controls and reporting;
- reusable checklists and templates.

## Out of scope

The POC does not provide legal advice, does not reproduce standards in full, and
does not claim to be an exhaustive conformance methodology. Document accessibility,
procurement and native mobile patterns are future extensions.
